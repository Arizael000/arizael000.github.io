Documental Report

This website was created at the request of Prysmian's Export Operations team to make visible the workflows dedicated to analyzing, reconciling, and reviewing PDF documents for Packing Lists and Invoices before proceeding with export procedures.

Before export clearance

3 stages
  1. 01
    Analyze

    Read and classify the Packing List and invoice PDFs.

  2. 02
    Reconcile

    Match quantities, values and references against the shipment record.

  3. 03
    Review

    Complete the final validation before the export procedure moves forward.

The dashboard uses the published hojaPublica data. Case labels are generated, and source IDs and shipment references are omitted.
